About Solly's Dessert & Cookies

Baked With Love in East London Since 2020

At Solly's, we believe one cookie can fix a whole day. What started in a small kitchen in East London with one oven and a family recipe, is now the town's favorite dessert spot baking 500+ fresh treats daily.

We use real butter, Belgian chocolate, farm eggs, and zero shortcuts. No preservatives. No pre-mix. Just honest baking that tastes like home.
